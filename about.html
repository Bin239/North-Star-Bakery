About North Star Bakery

Baking with patience, passion, and traditional techniques since 2014.

Our Story

North Star Bakery started in a small home kitchen with a single sourdough starter named "Northie." What began as a weekend passion project for neighborhood block parties quickly grew into a beloved community fixture.

In 2018, we opened our brick-and-mortar shop in Denver. While our ovens got bigger, our commitment remained unchanged: simple, high-quality ingredients transformed through time-honored artisanal methods.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

100% Regional Grains

We source our wheat and rye directly from family-owned mills in the region, supporting local agriculture while bringing exceptional flavor to our loaves.

Real Butter & Dairy

No shortcuts, artificial oils, or preservatives. Every croissant, cake, and Danish is baked using pure European-style cultured butter and organic milk.

Zero Waste Commitment

We donate day-old breads to local food banks daily, and all remaining organic food scraps are composted at neighboring community gardens.
